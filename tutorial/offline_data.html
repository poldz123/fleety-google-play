How to Download Transit Data for Offline Use

Commuting through underground subway tunnels or low-reception areas? Save complete schedule timetables directly to your device and lookup departure times anytime without cellular data or Wi-Fi.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Open Transit Operator Preferences

From the bottom navigation bar, navigate to the Transit tab or tap your operator selector at the top. Access the configuration menu for your active transit agency (e.g., OC Transpo, TTC, or GO Transit).

2

Enable "Download Transit Schedule Data"

Turn on the switch labeled "Download transit schedule data for offline use". Fleety immediately establishes a connection to fetch the latest complete GTFS schedule package.

3

Watch the Download Progress

A blue progress bar will indicate download and indexing progress. Once completed, a confirmation badge displays with the cached package size (typically 3 to 15 MB) and the date saved.

4

Browse Full Schedules Offline

You're ready to commute! Turn on Airplane Mode or enter an underground subway station—Fleety automatically serves stops, route sequences, and full daily timetables instantly from your phone's memory.

5

Manage Storage or Delete Anytime

Need to free up space or refresh seasonal schedules? Tap Delete or toggle the switch off to purge the offline cache from your phone with a single tap.

Pro Commuter Tips
  • Download on Wi-Fi Before Your Trip: Save data bandwidth by downloading your daily agency's offline schedule bundle while connected to home or office Wi-Fi.
  • Subway Tunnel Essential: In underground rail stations where cellular signals cut out, offline mode allows you to check transfer timetables without waiting for reception.
  • Seamless Auto-Fallback: You do not need to manually toggle anything when entering dead zones—Fleety falls back to your local cache automatically.
  • Lightweight Storage: Even large metropolitan transit networks are compressed into a compact file footprint (typically under 12 MB).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does offline mode show live moving buses on the map?

Live GPS tracking requires an active internet connection to receive real-time vehicle coordinates. In offline mode, Fleety displays the full planned scheduled arrival times.

How often should I re-download offline schedules?

Most transit operators revise schedules every month or each season. Re-downloading once a month ensures you have the most up-to-date timetable changes.

Can I download schedules for multiple transit agencies?

Yes! You can download offline schedules for each transit operator you have added to Fleety.
