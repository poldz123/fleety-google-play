How to Save & Manage Favorites in Fleety

Bookmark your daily bus routes, frequent stops, and specific schedules with a single tap of the star icon. Access everything in the Favorites tab, or tap the star again to remove it.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Tap the Star Icon on Any Screen

Whenever you are viewing a Route, a Stop, or an upcoming Schedule, look at the top-right corner of the screen. Tap the Star (⭐️) icon.

2

Check the Confirmation Toast

Fleety displays a bottom toast: Added to favorite. The star fills in solid to indicate that the item is saved.

3

Open the Favorites Tab

From the bottom navigation bar, tap the Favorites (⭐️) tab. Here, all your saved transit items are neatly grouped into three dedicated sub-tabs:

  • Schedules: Your most-watched departures with live minute countdowns.
  • Routes: Direct one-tap access to your daily bus and train lines.
  • Stops: Saved physical stop hubs showing all passing routes.
4

Remove a Favorite Anytime

Changed your routine? Simply tap the filled star icon again on that item's screen or use the edit pencil button in the Favorites tab to delete it instantly.

Pro Commuter Tips
  • Morning vs. Evening Commute: Star the departure for your morning stop heading downtown, and star the evening return stop. You can check both in seconds without searching!
  • Instant Offline Access: When you favorite an item, Fleety automatically caches its timetable and route coordinates on your device so you can view it even in subway tunnels.
  • One-Tap Route Timelines: Favoriting an entire route lets you open its complete stop timeline and destination switcher with a single tap right from the home screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a limit to how many items I can favorite?

No! You can save as many schedules, routes, and stops as you need across multiple transit operators.

Do favorites sync across devices?

Favorites are securely stored locally on your device for instant offline access and maximum battery efficiency.

Can I reorder my favorites?

Yes! Tap the blue pencil floating button in the bottom right of the Favorites screen to reorder or batch delete saved items.
