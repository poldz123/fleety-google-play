How to View Full Timetables & Calendar Schedules

Need to see all departure times for the day or plan a trip for next week? Tap "See All Schedules" to view the complete daily timetable, spot the last trip of the night, and select any calendar date.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Open the Route Schedule

From the Nearby or Transit tab, tap on the departure card for the route and stop you wish to view (for example, Route 51 Britannia at Carling / Richardson).

2

Tap "See All Schedules"

Scroll to the bottom of the upcoming departure list. Tap the "See All Schedules" button. This opens the dedicated Timetable screen.

3

Browse Organized Daily Departures

The Timetable groups all departure times by date (such as TODAY, TOMORROW, and future weekdays). The final trip of the night is marked with a prominent orange LAST tag.

4

Select Any Specific Calendar Date

Want to check the schedule for next Tuesday or an upcoming weekend? Tap the Calendar icon (📅) in the top-right corner, select your desired date from the calendar dialog, and tap OK. Fleety immediately loads the exact timetable for that day.

Pro Commuter Tips
  • Weekend & Holiday Schedules: Transit lines frequently run reduced frequencies or altered routes on Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays. Use the calendar picker ahead of time to verify changes before you head out.
  • Never Miss the Last Bus: When planning late-night trips or commutes after a concert or sports game, check the LAST badge so you know the exact cutoff time for your trip.
  • Easy Dismissal: Tap the X in the top-left corner anytime to return directly back to your active live map view.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance can I check timetables?

You can check dates as far ahead as the transit agency has published their seasonal service schedules (usually several weeks to months in advance).

Are timetable departures updated for live delays?

Immediate departures for today display real-time live minute countdowns based on GPS. Future departures show the agency's official scheduled timetable.

Can I see timetables without internet access?

Yes! Fleety pre-caches timetables on your device so you can view all-day departure times even in subway tunnels or areas with low cellular reception.
