How to Select & Manage Transit Operators in Fleety

Commute between multiple cities or regional transit systems? Search transit agencies by name, city, or country, add several operators at once, and toggle between them seamlessly with the top switcher.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Tap the Operator Selector

On the top navigation bar of Fleety, tap your currently active transit operator name (or the dropdown arrow). Tap the "+ Add/Remove Transit" button to open the operator directory.

2

Select Your Country or Region

Choose your country tab (such as Canada or United States) to focus on transit agencies operating in your region.

3

Search by City, Region, or Operator Name

Use the search input field to type a municipality (e.g. Toronto, Ottawa, Kingston, New York) or a specific agency abbreviation like TTC, GO, or MTA. Fleety filters results in real time.

4

Multi-Select Your Commuter Agencies

Check the checkbox next to every transit network you ride. You can select multiple operators at once (for example, combining local municipal buses with regional commuter rail). Uncheck any agencies you no longer use.

5

Switch Between Networks with One Tap

Confirm your selection. All chosen operators are now pinned to your quick-switch dropdown menu at the top of Fleety. Tap any operator name to instantly reload routes, stops, and live GPS vehicles for that system.

Pro Commuter Tips
  • Regional Commuters: If you take a commuter rail train (such as GO Transit) and transfer to a municipal subway/bus (such as TTC or MiWay), add both to your quick list for rapid transfers.
  • Favorites Stay Intact: When you switch operators, your bookmarked favorite routes and stops for other agencies are safely saved in your Favorites tab.
  • Travel Ready: Visiting another city? Add that city's transit provider before you depart, download offline schedules, and hit the ground running.
  • Suggest an Agency: Missing your hometown transit system? Email support@fleetytransit.com with your city name and we will look into adding it!

Frequently Asked Questions

How many transit agencies can I add to Fleety?

There is no limit! You can add as many municipal, regional, and rail operators as you need for daily commutes and travel.

Does Fleety support both Canada and the United States?

Yes! Fleety supports major transit authorities across Canadian provinces and US metropolitan areas, with new GTFS feeds continuously added.

Does switching agencies delete my offline data?

No. Offline schedule bundles remain cached per agency on your phone until you explicitly delete them in settings.
