How to Preview Stops Instantly with Hold-to-Preview

Check the exact location of any bus or train stop on the map in seconds. Press and hold any schedule card to zoom in, and release your finger to snap right back.

The Gesture at a Glance
1. Press & Hold

Touch and hold any schedule row to dynamically swoop down and focus on that stop.

2. Release Finger

Lift your finger to instantly bounce back to your neighborhood map without losing your spot.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Open Nearby or Transit

Launch Fleety on your iPhone or Android phone. On the bottom navigation bar, tap on Nearby to see live transit stops and upcoming departures around you.

2

Press & Hold the Schedule Item

Find the bus or train departure you want to check. Instead of a fast tap, press and hold your thumb down on the schedule card row.

3

Inspect the Stop Location on the Map

While your finger is held down, the map camera animates and zooms directly into the stop. You will see an orange target halo highlighting the exact curb, stop pole, and nearby cross-streets.

4

Release to Return to Your Map

Lift your finger when you’re done. The camera smoothly pulls back out to your full overview map. You stay right where you were in the schedule list, with zero back buttons needed.

Why This Gesture is a Game Changer
  • Which side of the intersection? Quickly verify whether your bus stops before or after crossing a busy intersection before you start walking.
  • Compare routes instantly: Hold on Route A to preview its stop, release, and hold on Route B to immediately see which one is closer to your current location.
  • No loading delays: Because stop coordinates are pre-cached on your device, the map zoom animation is instant with zero lag.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a normal tap do?

A regular quick tap opens the complete schedule view, live bus GPS tracking, and future arrival countdowns. Read the Full Schedule tutorial →

Does it work with my saved Favorites?

Yes! You can use hold-to-preview from the Nearby tab, search results, and your saved Favorites list.

Which cities and transit agencies support this?

Every city supported by Fleety! Whether you commute on TTC, OC Transpo, STM, MTA, CTA, or other agencies across Canada and the US.
