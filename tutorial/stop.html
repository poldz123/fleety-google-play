Search or browse transit stops by 4-digit stop code and walking proximity. View every route serving that curb, and tap any arrival time to jump right into the full live schedule screen.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Open the Stops Catalog

From the bottom navigation bar, tap on Transit, then switch to the Stops tab at the top. You'll see stops sorted by proximity, showing the stop name, distance from you (e.g., 155 m), and pills for every route that stops there.

2

Select a Stop

Tap on any stop card (such as Carling / Richardson with stop code #2132). Fleety smoothly opens the dedicated Stop Screen.

3

Review All Serving Routes

Instead of checking routes one-by-one, the Stop Screen lists all upcoming departures from that physical curb (for example, Route 85 Bayshore arriving in 1 min and Route 51 Britannia arriving in 4 min).

4

Tap an Arrival Time to Open Full Schedule

Want to track where that approaching bus is on the map? Simply tap on the arrival time row (e.g., 85 Bayshore • 1 min). Fleety instantly redirects you to the full Schedule Screen with the live vehicle map and future departure times!

Pro Commuter Tips
  • Catch Whichever Bus Comes First: If two different bus routes both take you to your destination (such as a central subway station), open the Stop Screen to see which one will arrive first.
  • 4-Digit Stop Codes: Every bus pole on the street has a red or yellow ID number. Look for this matching number on the red flag icon in Fleety (e.g. 2132) to guarantee you're waiting at the correct stop.
  • Star Your Stop: Tap the Star (⭐️) icon in the top right of the Stop Screen to pin the entire stop hub to your Favorites.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are the stops sorted in the Transit tab?

Stops are automatically sorted by straight-line proximity to your current GPS position, showing the closest stops first with distance meters.

What happens when I tap the back button on the Schedule screen?

Tapping the Close (X) button brings you right back to the Stop Screen so you can check other routes without starting over.

Can I see stops for other transit agencies?

Yes! Tap the operator dropdown at the top of the Transit tab (e.g. OC Transpo, TTC, STM) to view stops for any agency.
